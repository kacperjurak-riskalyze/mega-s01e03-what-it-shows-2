The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first. A day-first tenant books 7 April and the invite says 4 July. Most of #4471 is this.

hint: booking-v3 prints an MM/DD/YYYY hint to day-first tenants. Their people type month first, as the hint says, and the form reads the date day first. The dates come out swapped, so it looks like parser, but the parser fix does not touch it. It shows only through the form channel at tenants that run booking-v3.

move: the invite moves by one day. No month is flipped: the 12 May invite shows 11 May, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday while one in Toronto gets Monday. That points at time zones, not at date order. The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but the cause table on #4471 counts them under parser.

How the reports were placed

  • One id is one report. The export comes in overlapping pages, so some rows appear twice; the counts drop the repeats.
  • The cause comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form (#lib/kb/causes), not from the intake tag. The tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser.
  • Counts are the desk’s own, on the export as it is. The one-day moves stay counted under the cause they are filed under, and also get the move row and ticket (#lib/kb/problems).
